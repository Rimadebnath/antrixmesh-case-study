# AntrixMesh: Business Analysis & Product Ownership Case Study

How a mid-build request to "go fully Web3" became a feasibility check, a trade-off the client could decide on, and a two-phase plan: Phase 1 shipped on time and was paid for, and Phase 2 became a Web3 trial.

**Live case study:** https://antrixmesh-case-study.vercel.app

- **Author:** Rima Debnath
- **Company:** Captur Advisors (internship, Aug 2021 – Jan 2022)
- **Role:** Front-end lead and acting product owner: requirements, research, client alignment and sign-offs
- **Team:** 4 (2 developers, 2 research/legal analysts)
- **Product:** AntrixMesh, a crypto news heat-map showing where in the world crypto news is happening

## What the case study covers

- **The pivot:** checking feasibility, an impact analysis on time, cost, scope, risk and skill gaps (options A/B/C), and the two-phase roadmap the client chose
- **Research:** about 15 conversations with crypto users, NFT collectors and crypto-news readers, and what each finding changed
- **Requirements:** a requirements excerpt, a MoSCoW backlog, user stories with acceptance criteria, a use case with alternate flows, and a traceability table
- **Delivery:** two-week Scrum sprints on a Kanban board, a change-request log, and the Phase 1 sign-off
- **Design:** the reasoning behind the heat map, news card and wallet sign-in, plus an interactive prototype of the Phase 2 site

All screens are redrawn for the case study and labelled as redrawn.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single page, with no frameworks. Hosted on Vercel.

The original AntrixMesh code (React, Leaflet, Python) is at https://github.com/Rimadebnath/AntrixMesh.
